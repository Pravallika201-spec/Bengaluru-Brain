System Overview

The system divides Bengaluru into predictive grid cells and identifies locations requiring prioritized waste collection.

Collection priorities are generated using predicted waste generation and operational risk indicators.

Trucks are dynamically assigned to high-priority locations and routes are sequenced to reduce unnecessary travel.
